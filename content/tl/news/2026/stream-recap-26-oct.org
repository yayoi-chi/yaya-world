There was once a group of four cats. One cat convinced two of the other cats to entered the forbidden ritual storehouse. You see the cats always wanted to see what was inside, but their owner (Rika-chamma) was mean I didn't let them in. They took a peek though and now the cats are scared. Don't worry cats, just keep meowing. Rika-chamma isn't mad, she'll protect you!
