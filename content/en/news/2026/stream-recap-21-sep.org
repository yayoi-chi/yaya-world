** Danganronpa 2
So apparently the strawberry house is just one side of the funhouse. There's a lift that goes between the two sides and we found out that these two sides are not only very close, but they're also connected by the tower in the middle. It also seems the phone in the lounges of each side can be used to communicate. So things might just get interesting. Also, no announcements, but we still have free time. How will this change things?
