#+SUMMARY: So, I couldn't spend my birthday with my kittens, but we did one better! I celebrated the entire week of my birthday with them. We had lots of fun with two art streams. We headed back to the White Palace where I actually made some progress. We got back Paznos. We even completed another chapter in Danganronpa 2. I had lots of fun and I hope the kittens did too!

* Art Streams
Panel three of the comic turned out a bit more complicated. I still hope that I can continue to hone my process and maybe make these a little faster. Still we finished the panel and only have one panel left! Can't wait to show this off!
* Game Streams
Started this week's gaming streams with Hollow Knight. I made some good progress in the White Palace! We continued with Dark Cloud 2. I was very happy to find out there was only one red seal. You know these wouldn't be a problem if they didn't give us such big enemies. The final stream of my birthday week was a VN Nite. We finally finished this class trial and the chapter!
** Hollow Knight
After a looong break trying to get to Nod-Krai in Genshin, we returned to Hollownest and the White Palace. Yes this place is hard. There aren't any enemies per se, but plenty of things that try to keel chu. Still, it didn't take me long to get back into things and I impressed myself with some of my platforming. We made it to the elevator and passed some "enemies" and even found a new bench. Not sure how much more we have left. I just know we are going to avoid the Path of Pain 😝
