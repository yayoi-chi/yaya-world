It was oops all gacha games 😝 No, really. Most of the games we played this week were gacha games. The only exception, in fact, was Dark Cloud 2. We're almost done with that one. We made it to Startorch Academy. If we were hoping for a normal school life, well, that's not going to happen apparently. So yeah, not exactly a normal day. Well, let's see how the rest of my school life will be here!
