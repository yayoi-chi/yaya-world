* Art Streams
I got a special chibi request thanks to my redeems on Twitch. I got to draw Twinwhitetiger as a moogle. We made the moogle special in the way only Twin could. It has stripes, pastel stripes, and pink wings and a pink pom pom. I can say there aren't many moogles like this. Due to some space constraints from syncing my YouTube content to Odysee, I wasn't able to work on a timelapse of this, but I'll get that done as soon as I can!
* Game Streams
We participated in the totally adorable little witches' event in Genshin Impact. Prune acts all mature while Yaoyao, Qiqi, Sayu, and Klee embrace their inner child. That wasn't the only cute thing though, we also helped Lacrimosa with her image problem. It was cuteness overload for the first half of the week for sure. We got a break from that with some Super Mario Bros. 3 racing practice and Danganronpa 2, though Danganronpa did have /some/ cute moments!
** Genshin Impact
We still had some story quests and even a little side quest to take care of, but I think we are ready to move forward in the Nod-Krai story. That, however, will have to wait until next time. You see, my main goal was to get Prune, but while I was looking for her trial I noticed an event. Prune and the Little Witches had a mystery to solve and an imaginary friend to help! It was so adorable, I just had to play it. We'll finish it up and get back to archon quests next time!
